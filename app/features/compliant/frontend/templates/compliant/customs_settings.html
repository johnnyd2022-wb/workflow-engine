Products

Each workflow's final product: its Customs product type, pack size and strength.

Loading products…

ABV comes from each batch's final step; the fallback is used only when a batch has none. A tariff item override replaces the built-in rate for that product.

Duty rates

Built in from Customs' duties table and updated every 1 July.

Override a rate

Only for a tariff item Customs has given you a different rate for. Per litre of alcohol, from its start date.

Lodging

How often you lodge with Customs, and from when the monthly system check reminds you.

With no date, reminders start from the month the NZ Alcohol module was switched on. Changing how often you lodge needs Customs' approval first.

Stocktake

How often you count. Compliance raises a system check before each one is due.

Bottles must match exactly. Bulk liquid within the tolerance counts as matching. Customs expects at least one stocktake a year.
