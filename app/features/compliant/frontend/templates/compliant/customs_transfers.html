CCAs you send to

Link a customer to its CCA and every sale to them is a transfer, left out of duty.

Goods leaving your CCA are either moved to another CCA or entered for duty. For each move Customs asks for the destination, the date it moved, the carrier, the date it arrived and what moved, and the destination's written acknowledgement that it arrived (Customs operational policy, Product movement excise unpaid – alcohol, paragraphs 15–16). Until then the goods are still your responsibility.
