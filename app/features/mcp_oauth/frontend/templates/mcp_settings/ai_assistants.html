Access

Off until you turn it on. Turning it off stops every connected assistant straight away; they work again if you turn it back on.

Some of what an assistant reads comes from outside your team: customer and supplier names and invoice text from Xero. Biz-E marks the fields it knows come from outside in each answer, and tells the assistant they are data, never instructions. Messages and notes your contract customers write in their portal are never shared with assistants.

Who can connect one

Ticked roles can connect their own assistant. It reads only what that person can see.

Tick at least one role. Until you do, nobody can connect an assistant.
